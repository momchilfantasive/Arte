06 / Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Our team is happy to help.

Ask a questionAsk a question

Paper blinds, tension rods with lightweight curtains and adhesive frosted films work well for short-term needs. They are cost-effective, easy to install and give adequate privacy and light control.

Roller blinds, minimalist tracks and automated blinds are among today's most popular choices. Neutral tones, clean lines and innovative materials lead contemporary designs, combining style with everyday function.

Consider Roman blinds, layered curtains or plantation shutters. They bring out the architecture of a bay window while giving you control over light and privacy.

Think about access and durability first. Sliding panels or vertical blinds suit busy areas, while classic curtains add insulation and help with energy efficiency.
