## Defects (by severity)

### D1 — Major · S06 "Previous project" does nothing after scrolling past cards
- **Where:** home page, projects stack. Fails on desktop-1440, ipad-1180 and ipad-1366 (the phone hides the buttons).
- **Repro:** open `/`, scroll to the projects stack, press *Next project* twice (P1 → P2 → P3), then press *Previous project*. **Expected** P2, **actual** stays on P3. Next works; Previous after pinning does not.
- **Cause (diagnosed, not fixed):** `src/js/modules/projects.js` `go()` calls `lenis.scrollTo(cards[i])`. Pinned and released cards all report `top ≈ 0`, so the target equals the current scroll position. Fix: scroll to each card's stored ScrollTrigger `start` instead of the element.
- **Evidence:** spec `qa/specs/s06-projects.spec.js:72`; traces and video in `qa/artifacts/test-results/*Previous*` (local).

### D2 — Minor (accessibility) · Reduced motion is only partly honoured
- **Where:** reduced-1180.
- **Repro:** turn on *Reduce Motion* in the OS, then load `/`. The full preloader still runs (**7.97 s** measured; the expectation is under 3 s). Hero parallax still moves (`translateY 287px` after scroll).
- **What works:** text and section reveals show immediately (opacity 1, transform none).
- **Recommendation:** under `prefers-reduced-motion`, use a short fade preloader (or skip it) and disable parallax and the hero zoom. Do not change the default choreography.

### D3 — Observation · Cookie banner and theme switcher cover hero/card content
- On phone-390 the cookie banner covers the hero CTA area. On iPad it sits over the project-card title and Prev/Next area until dismissed (see the S01/S06 contact sheets).
- It is a one-time banner, so this is low impact. But it is the first thing a client sees on the preview. The DARK/LIGHT switcher is a temporary review tool and will be removed after the theme decision.

### Not a defect · `/showroom-interiors.html` 404 on ipad-1180 (1 of 4 viewports)
- One response returned 404 during the run. It did not reproduce: re-running the same test passed, and `curl` returns 200.
- Most likely cause: `dist/` was rebuilt by a diagnostic build while the run was using it. Classified as a **harness artifact**. If it shows up again, check whether the preview server was rebuilt mid-run.

## Engine differences and limits
- **Video codec:** Chromium here has no H.264, so it played the `.webm` fallbacks. Safari will play the **MP4** sources, and that path is unverified. `ERR_ABORTED` on media range requests is recorded but treated as benign.
- **WebKit:** not run (see above). Safari-specific risks are not covered: momentum and touch scrolling through GSAP pins, the dynamic address bar or `100vh`, video autoplay rules, and Low Power Mode.
- **Touch** is emulated (`hasTouch`). Real finger scrolling and inertia are not reproduced.
- Headless timing under CPU load is slower than a real device. Durations are upper bounds.

## Recommended priorities
1. **D1 Prev button.** Small, contained fix in `projects.js`, and highly visible on iPad.
2. **D2 reduced motion.** Short preloader and no parallax under reduce. About 20 lines; no change to the default choreography.
3. **Manual Safari pass** (checklist below) on a real iPad, Mac and iPhone. This is the main gap the harness cannot close.
4. **D3** together with the theme decision: once the switcher goes, re-check where the cookie banner sits.
5. Harness follow-ups: run the dark theme (`QA_THEME=dark`), and add WebKit on a Mac.
